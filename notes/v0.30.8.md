Every card casts its own shadow.

The clipboard strip is separate cards in one window, and a window's
shadow is cut from the union of whatever in it is opaque. On a pale
ground the shadow's rim drew a hairline around that union, bridging
the gaps between the pin column, the row, and a menu, a line the dark
side never showed. The window casts no shadow now; each card casts
its own, shaped to its own rounded rectangle rather than to whatever
the glass happens to draw.
