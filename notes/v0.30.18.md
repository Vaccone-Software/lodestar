The pinch arrives.

The image door zoomed for nobody: macOS sends a pinch to the active
application, and Lodestar is never active, so the gesture went to
whatever app stood in front and the door never saw it. It listens now
the way the strip listens for a click, through a watcher live only
while the door stands, and zooms about the point under the pointer. Two
fingers tapped twice go to one point per pixel from the fitted size, and
back to fitted from anywhere else.
