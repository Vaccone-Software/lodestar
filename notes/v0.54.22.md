A release that makes Lodestar steadier: its language models now run apart from it, updates no longer stall, and copies that used to slip past Keep are kept.

The models behind the editor and dictation now run in a separate part of Lodestar that macOS manages, the way Apple has apps keep heavy work apart from themselves. A model that stops answering is now ended and started fresh at the next sentence, where it used to leave grammar silent until Lodestar restarted. When a model is let go, its memory goes with it at once, and quitting can no longer crash inside the model's code.

Updates no longer pause for five seconds while the old Lodestar finishes. Its records' upkeep now waits until it has settled and is the copy that stays, so it never holds up a quit.

A copy caught at the moment its app had cleared the clipboard but not yet written it, or before its app had handed over its contents, is now read again a moment later instead of being missed. A copy made while the screen was locked, such as one from your phone, is now kept once you unlock. Every copy now leaves a line in Lodestar's log saying whether it was kept or refused and which app it came from, never what it said, so a copy that does not reach Keep can be traced.
