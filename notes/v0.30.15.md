The first weeks are a curriculum.

The walk taught seven gestures in a minute, and a hand that has seen
seven owns none of them. It now teaches three and stops: the key, the
launcher, a few letters. Everything else arrives later, on the same
card, one lesson at a time, on a calendar the hand sets. Hints on the
second day, Ask on the fourth, then the clipboard, the cheat sheet,
the draft, select, commands, and scroll, each only if the gesture has
never once fired, each proved by doing it once. A lesson rides the same
quiet the coach's chip does and yields to any suggestion with evidence
behind it. Lode delete means later; a lesson returns once, days on,
then parks for good. A gesture the hand found on its own is never
taught.
