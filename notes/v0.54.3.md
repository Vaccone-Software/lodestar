The draft no longer gives up on the microphone. When it would not start, the draft said so and waited, and the only way to ask again was to close it and open it again. Now it asks again on its own, after a second, then two, then four, then every five seconds for as long as the draft is open, and the line under it says it is trying again. `lode .` asks at once. Everything already in the draft stays where it is, including the words the recognizer was still settling when the microphone went. If you have muted the draft, it waits for you.

A headset that will not start no longer ends a take. With the lid open, the Mac's own microphone hears you while a Bluetooth headset wakes. When the headset failed to start, the draft threw that away along with what you were saying. The Mac's microphone now carries the rest of the take, and a headset that comes up late takes over the way a slow one always has. A microphone that stops partway through a take is asked for again too, instead of leaving the draft listening to nothing.
