A plain summon now leaves nothing of its own behind it.

The resting posture has always been one app, maximized — but that was
true of the layout, not of the screen. Windows you had summoned earlier
could still be standing behind the one you just brought forward, which
anyone with a transparent terminal knows intimately: the glass shows
Slack when it should show wallpaper. Now a plain summon sweeps them —
every window Lodestar placed this session that is still standing on the
active display parks with it, silently, because the screen changing is
the feedback.

The scope is the entire design, and it is why there is no setting. Only
windows this session was asked to move are ever moved unasked: something
you summoned, adopted with `lode 0`, or restored through a breath. A
window macOS placed — a second window you opened by hand, a dialog, a
stray from before the restart — stays exactly where its app put it, the
promise this app has kept from the beginning. If a straggler bothers
you behind the glass, `lode 0` was always the adoption gesture, and one
press brings it into the system; every summon after that sweeps it.

An earlier Lodestar had a sweep and retired it. That one was a verb that
parked every non-member window, and its flaw was structural: it had to
guess what was safe to move, and guessed at dialogs. This one never
guesses — its jurisdiction is exactly the list of windows you have
already trusted it with — which is what lets it run on every plain
summon without asking anyone anything.

Two guarantees ride with it. The sweep belongs to the summon's own undo
step, so one `lode ←` restores the whole stage — members and swept alike,
each window back where it stood. And any road to a parked window that
Lodestar does not own — cmd-tab, a Dock click — unparks it on the spot,
back at its remembered frame. Nothing this app hides can be lost to a
gesture it does not hear. That guarantee is new for all parking, not
just the sweep: the window an old summon displaced comes back the same
way now, which closes a small trap that predates this release.
