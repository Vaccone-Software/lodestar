Every letter belongs to the graph.

Layout undo moves to lode ← and lode →. If your hands know lode Z, that is
the one thing to relearn; nothing else you type changes, and your config
needs no edit. Undo and redo now read as a pair the way lode [ and lode ]
already do, and walking a history is what the arrows have always meant.

The attention timeline — lode X back, lode ⇧X forward, through the windows
you had been in — is retired rather than moved. It was the least-used
gesture in the grammar by a wide margin, and once your chains are fluent it
answers a question the graph answers better: lode G reaches Ghostty in a
tenth of a second and tells you exactly where you will land, which a
back-jump cannot promise. The letter was worth more than the verb.

That is the point of both changes. A verb sitting on a letter costs you an
address for as long as it sits there, and addresses are the scarcer thing —
there are twenty-six, and eventually you want all of them. As of this
release none are spoken for. Zoom can be lode Z. Xcode can be lode X.

Several places had quietly disagreed about which letters were reserved, and
the disagreement had teeth: switching off the orientation flip disabled lode
O instead, so turning off a verb that went on working cost you an address
that did not. There is one list now, and everything reads from it.

The coach can see further too. It reads a browser profile's name properly
for the first time — "Brave (Work)" was being read as "Brave" and nothing
more, so a profile could never be offered a shorter address however often
you reached for it. And the arithmetic behind judging whether a shorter
chain is worth learning had a flaw that pinned every such suggestion just
under the bar, no matter how much evidence stood behind it. Both are fixed,
so the first suggestions of this kind will begin arriving: a deep chain you
lean on, and the single letter it has earned.
