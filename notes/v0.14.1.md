The advisor learns from its first real day.

One day of genuine use taught the analysis five lessons, and this release
applies them. Wrong key evidence lives at the prefix where the hand
stumbled, often an internal letter of the graph, so the advisor now reads
the whole trie rather than its leaves: the strongest confusion in the first
real dataset was invisible to the leaf reading. The letters the grammar
reserves for verbs, O and X and Z, can never be offered as addresses; a
suggestion the write path would refuse should not exist. A clicked link
that matched no rule is evidence of nothing, so pass through opens no
longer count toward, or against, a route suggestion.

Two calibrations for how attention actually concentrates. When most of a
day lives in three applications, lift is bounded low and the old breath
gate could never fire on the user's most obvious pattern; the evidence now
rides the count against what independence predicts, and a pair is one
suggestion however many directions it was measured in. Clustering gained
the same null model: applications group only when their connection beats
what their sheer traffic predicts, which turns one cluster of everything
into the two or three working sets that are actually there.

And the recall mixture stays silent when a day shows no reconstruction
mode at all, rather than fabricating ownership numbers from an unimodal
cloud; the blind rate carries fluency until there is a second mode to
find.
