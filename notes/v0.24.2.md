Menu search grows into the commands bar.

`lode .` keeps its key and its manner, and takes a truer name: the
commands bar, where the focused app's verbs answer to a few typed
letters. The rename reaches everything that says the word — the
cheat sheet, the settings row, the guide — and a config that still
says menu-search reads as commands without being asked twice.

The name is also a foundation. Every row now knows its source, and a
list that mixes feeds will wear a chip saying whose verb each row
is. System commands join as a second feed in a coming release, and
at equal match they will outrank an app's three hundred menu items
on purpose: a dozen curated verbs must never drown.

One honest fix rode along. A row without a native shortcut wore a
small empty stub where its chip would have been; rows now learn they
are empty, and say nothing instead.
