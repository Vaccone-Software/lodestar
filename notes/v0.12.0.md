The searcher is called the launcher, and a summon arrives maximized.

One vocabulary now, in the app, the reference and the product page. What `lode
space` opens is a **launcher**, because that is the word people reach for when
they describe it without being prompted, and because "a list of your
applications" is what it actually is. What a summon does to a window is
**maximize** it, which is the word the app already used for `lode 0` and which
does not collide with what macOS calls full screen. The teaching surfaces are
the whole design bet here, so the words on them have to be the words you would
use out loud.

`gestures.searcher` in a config written before this release is read as
`gestures.launcher`, and the count of launcher summons in your observations
moves rather than resetting. Neither had to break to be renamed.

The README no longer opens by asking whether you enjoy managing windows.
Nobody ever sat down wanting to, so the premise was arguing with a position its
reader had never held. It says what Lodestar does instead: navigates to the
application you want, arriving maximized, with everything else out of the way
and ordinary windows still behaving the way they always did.

Two additions to the debug harness, both dropped from release builds. A capture
stage (`LODESTAR_STAGE=1`) gives every panel a flat ground to be photographed
against, which is what the product page's images are shot on, and it exists
because a glass panel captured over a terminal has that terminal's text inside
it. And the Ask bar gained a preview that types into the real field rather than
a copy of it, over a synthetic config, so a screenshot of it can never carry
somebody's own browser profile names.
