Reduce loud audio no longer locks the volume at silence. AirPods can report a flat volume curve for a moment as they connect or change mode, and that left a ceiling of zero that the keys and the slider could not move. A curve is now trusted only once it rises from silent to full, and it is read again as the headphones settle. A volume-up press that the ceiling stops now sets the volume to the ceiling and unmutes, so the keys reach as far as the slider.

Every Bluetooth headset is now held on one curve, so Light, Standard and Strong mean the same level on AirPods as on any other headphones. Wired headphones keep the Mac's own curve.

Calls can play louder. While a meeting, chat or browser app is using the microphone, the limit sits 12 dB higher, because speech in a call plays well under the loudest music. The volume itself is never raised, only the limit, and when the call ends a volume above the usual limit is brought back down.

Every program Lodestar runs is now started one way, through Stoic 0.9.1. When an update step is stopped at its deadline, anything it started stops with it. The checks Lodestar makes with launchd have a deadline too, so a busy launchd can no longer hold up the app. Helpers that outlive Lodestar, such as the update watchdog, no longer inherit its open files or its signal settings.
