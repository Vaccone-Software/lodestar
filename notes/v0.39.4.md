Dictation starts listening sooner on a Bluetooth headset.

Most drafts start listening in about a seventh of a second. The slow
ones were all the same: a Bluetooth headset, which has to leave the
mode it plays music in for the one it takes calls in before it can hear
you, and that takes one to two and a half seconds, sometimes more.

Now, with the lid open, the Mac's own microphone listens while the
headset wakes, from the moment the draft opens, and hands over to the
headset as soon as the headset hears your voice. Measured on a headset
that took between a second and a quarter and six seconds to open, the
draft was listening in under half a second. The headset is still the
microphone the draft names, and nothing it hears or fails to hear is
held against it on the Mac's account.

With the lid closed nothing changes: the Mac's microphone is off then,
and the draft waits for the headset as it always has.
