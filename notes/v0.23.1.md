Select mode learns what it is actually for. A new setting,
select.copy-on-complete, serves the clipboard the moment a span's
second anchor lands — in every ending alike, native selection or held
highlight — with a quiet "copied" on the band as the receipt. The
highlight stands exactly as it always did and ⌘C still works; the
keystroke just stops being necessary.

This is not the auto-copy the design once buried. That one inspected
the text and decided intent from its kind — a read-the-situation step
inside a fixed gesture. This one fires at the grammar's own full
stop, the second capital, after which nothing else can follow — and
only because a config line said so. The mode never arbitrates; the
file does, once, the way every decision here is moved out of the
moment of use. Off by default, one toggle in Settings under
Interaction. The single-anchor word keeps its ⌘C: a first anchor may
still be on its way to becoming a span, and firing there would be the
old mistake back again.
