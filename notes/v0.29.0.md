Letters under one hold compose.

Lode is the one key this product holds, and every letter is a tap, so
the phrase is the hold. While lode stays down the first letter takes
the stage as any summon does, and every letter after it joins beside:
`lode G B` is Ghostty beside Brave, `lode G B S` three columns, and
lifting lode ends the phrase. It is the beside verb spoken without the
shift, on addresses the hand already owns, so there is nothing new to
learn, and the whole phrase is one undo. Digits keep their meaning
under a hold, since moving inside a split is their job, and a chain
still chains. What it does not change is the summon: the next letter
of anything collapses the split back to one app, as it always has.
The record counts every composition, how long its split survives, and
a chord undone within two seconds — the correction made under one
hold, if that turns out to be a habit.

The copy is read twice. Select aims by pixels and that stays; what it
must not do is let the pixels have the last word on what leaves the
clipboard, because recognition wobbles on `1` and `l`, `0` and `O`,
and a silent swap in an identifier is the worst kind of wrong. Now a
held span's pixels are served at once, and then the app is asked to
select the same span by a short synthetic drag and copy it. When its
text agrees with the pixels up to those glyphs, the app's copy is the
truth and the mode ends natively, the app's own highlight the ending.
The drag never lands on a control, both ends are checked by role
first, and a drag that grabbed a neighbor is overruled. Where the app
cannot select, the span is read again from the frozen frame, cropped
and upscaled with the recognizer's alternatives consulted and numbers
repaired by shape. The log now says which reading served each copy,
and what the accessibility grounding said, so a week of use will show
where the wobble was.

And the product learns to get smaller. A gesture the hand has not
fired in ninety days becomes a finding in the report, named with the
one config line that would retire it, and the cheat sheet draws its
row quiet with the same line in its label. Nothing turns off on its
own and no chip ever carries it: the offer lives where the eye already
reads, and the accept is the line.
