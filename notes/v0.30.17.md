The image door fills the display, and the name is yours to edit.

An image opened above the strip yesterday, and the strip was only in the
way of the picture: an image being looked at lands nowhere, unlike a text
card being edited. So E on an image card now opens it across the whole
display, the strip gone beneath it until escape brings it back. It opens
fitted, the whole picture first, and from there the trackpad does what
it does to any image: a pinch zooms about the pointer, two fingers pan,
and a picture smaller than its frame stays centered at every size. No
slider and no buttons. And the save band opens with its offered name as
text rather than as a placeholder, the caret at its end, so a name is
edited rather than retyped; command delete clears it for one of your own.
