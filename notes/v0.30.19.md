The door takes focus, and gives it back.

A pinch reaches the active application and nowhere else, not the window
under the pointer and not a watcher outside it, so a door that stayed
behind another app zoomed for nobody, twice. The image door is now the
one surface in Lodestar that takes focus. The strip and the draft never
do, because what they do lands in the app behind them; the door's result
lands nowhere but the picture, so taking focus costs the hand nothing.
The app it took focus from is remembered and gets it back the moment the
door closes.
