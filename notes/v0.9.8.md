The download is a destination.

Lodestar now installs the way a Mac app should: the site offers a
notarized DMG, the pane draws the northern sky, and one drag along the
transfer arc puts the star in Applications. The disk image is drawn
from source like the icon — the same compass mark, the same sparse sky,
signed, notarized, and stapled in its own right. The app binary is now
universal, Apple silicon and Intel from the same artifact. The zip
remains, and remains the Homebrew lane; nothing moves for existing
installs.

Two releases that never left the pad ship alongside.

From 0.9.6, the searcher writes the graph: ⌘K on a searcher row opens a
glass card that adds the selected app to the graph — the chain
validated live against the trie — or removes a bound one, and every
comment in lodestar.yaml survives the surgical edit. The menu bar
learns Edit Config… and Reveal Config in Finder. app.disabled-gestures
gives way to a gestures: section of per-verb switches, the generated
template ships empty and validates clean, and a visible cheat sheet now
closes on Escape.

From 0.9.7, one browser was always a family: the profile plumbing
generalizes to the Chromium family. brave, chrome, and edge each scan
their own Local State, match their own title suffix, and launch through
their own app — declared under profiles, addressed as browser:name in
the graph, validated for collisions across browsers. Arc is
deliberately absent: it does not stamp profiles into window titles, so
summoning could never find its windows honestly.
