The key at the center gets its true name: lode.

What was hyper is now lode — the key you hold to speak to Lodestar.
The word is the product's own root: a lodestar is the star that
shows the way, and lode is the way itself. Hold the way key, and
every window is a word away.

Nothing about the gesture changes. The trigger is still right ⌘
(configurable at lode.trigger), the shim forms are still accepted,
and every verb, chain, and mode reads exactly as before. The guides,
the cheat sheet, and the documentation now all speak the same name.

Configs written before this release keep working: a hyper section
reads as lode automatically, and the next write upgrades the name in
place. The raw-hyper trigger value keeps its spelling — it names the
classic ⌘⌃⌥⇧ chord itself, which was never ours to rename.
