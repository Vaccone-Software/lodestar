The coach stops offering a breath you already have, and a breath used
now leaves a trace.

A saved breath that holds both apps of a pair answers the finding. The
coach reads every saved layout, not just its letters, and a pair it
already holds is tested but never offered, whichever letter it lives at
and whichever order the switches ran. The letter is the hand's to choose;
the combination is what the coach cares about, and a combination that
exists has nothing left to say but "use it".

A pair is named in one order. The ledger once keyed a breath by whichever
direction dominated the fortnight, so a breath accepted as one app and
another could return a week later as the other and the one, a brand-new
finding with the same two windows. Now both directions are one entry.

Restoring a breath is recorded, and the record names every app in the
layout it brought back. Before this a breath left no mark in the log at
all: the coach could offer one, watch it be accepted, and never learn
whether it was used. The use travels with the apps rather than the
letter, so rebinding a breath to a new key keeps its history.
