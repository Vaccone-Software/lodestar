The coach, repaced around a distinction it was missing: a chip that
was answered and a chip that merely stood are different events, and
only the first should buy days of silence.

The old floor put three days between any two offers, counted from the
showing rather than the answer — so one thirty-second chip nobody
read locked the whole channel until the weekend. Answers still buy
three quiet days, and no two chips will ever share a day, but an
unanswered suggestion now retries in two — the debut in one, because
the first chip of the channel's life is the one most likely to be
missed outright. Parking softened the same way: three unanswered
showings sleep a month now instead of two, since nobody said no to
them. The chip itself stands a full minute, and a showing only counts
against a suggestion's budget once it has stood ten seconds with
someone there to read it.

The missed-offer inbox keeps its promise. A chip that faded used to
survive in the menu for half an hour at most, until the next refresh
swept it; it now waits there until answered, parked, or outgrown.
Reading it from the menu costs the suggestion nothing — a viewing you
asked for is not an interruption.

Silence became auditable. Every hold the coach takes leaves one line
in the log naming itself, every change of what stands says why, and
`lodestar observations` gained a pacing line: when the coach last
spoke, what stands now, and the earliest it may speak again.

And the epoch ledger stopped believing wounded parses. The night
0.22.0 arrived, the outgoing binary read the incoming config format,
could not resolve three profile bindings, and stamped three removals
that never happened — restarting learning curves the hands had
honestly earned. Graph diffs now run only against the last cleanly
parsed graph; a parse that dropped leaves holds the baseline and says
so in the log.
