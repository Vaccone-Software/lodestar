Select's band comes back from under the bottom of the screen.

Select draws a band along the bottom of the display: what you have typed,
how many matches are left, what ⇧letter is about to do. Since 0.16.0 that
band has been sitting below the bottom edge of the screen. On a monitor you
saw the top rim of it and no words; on a laptop display it was gone
altogether. Nothing was wrong with the band.

Panels became titled windows in 0.16.0 so that Liquid Glass would take its
tone from the app rather than sniff the wallpaper behind each panel — the
change that made light mode hold. macOS answers a titled window by sliding
it down until its title bar clears the menu bar, and select hands its
overlay a whole display on purpose, menu bar strip included. So the entire
overlay was pushed down by exactly the height of the menu bar, and the band,
the one piece pinned to the overlay's own bottom, went off the screen with
it. Everything else is placed from where the overlay actually landed, which
is why the highlights and the chips stayed where your eyes expected them and
only the band looked broken.

The overlay keeps the display it was given now. The band is back, and the
top strip of the screen is covered again, so text up there can be
highlighted and labelled like everything else. Hints was one full-screen
window away from the same fault and gets the same footing. Both bands also
step up over the Dock now, by exactly what the Dock reserves on whichever
display they are on, instead of coming to rest on top of it.
