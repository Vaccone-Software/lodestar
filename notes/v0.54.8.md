A mouse wheel can turn the other way again, and this time Lodestar does it itself. macOS has one setting for scroll direction, shared by the trackpad and every mouse, so a page that follows your fingers on glass runs backwards under a wheel. Settings now has a place called Input, where Keys used to be, and its last group is Mice. Turn a mouse's wheel while the place is open and the mouse appears there by name, its row lit while the wheel moves. Switch it on and that wheel scrolls the other way, from the very first notch. The trackpad is never listed and never touched.

Each scroll is turned only when something vouches for where it came from. Either the mouse's own wheel report arrived a moment before it, which needs Input Monitoring, or an app such as Logi Options+ posted it, in which case it is listed as Mice through that app. A scroll nobody vouches for passes exactly as macOS sent it, so the worst a mistake can do is leave a wheel as it was. A mouse you switch on is kept by name in the config. One you leave off is forgotten when Lodestar quits.
