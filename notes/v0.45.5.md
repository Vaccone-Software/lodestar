When Lodestar speaks about itself, it now shows its mark, and the mark
fills as an update arrives. Lodestar has also stopped chiming at you
while it changes hands.

Ask for an update and the note that says a newer Lodestar is on its way
stays up while it downloads. The star beside it takes your accent one
point at a time, from a quiet stone to whole, so you can see how far it
has come without a bar or a number. Whole, the new Lodestar takes over,
and the star is there again to say it has.

For a few versions, Lodestar could ding several times in a row as it
started, then fall quiet. The new copy was opening in front of your work
for the few seconds before it took over, and every key that reached it
in that moment, with nowhere to go, was answered with the alert. It now
starts behind whatever you are doing, so the keys go where you meant
them and the handover is silent.
