The strip and the settings window speak the language.

Three faces, one per speaker, and a face is never borrowed: the
interface in the system's sans, the hand in mono, Lodestar in New York.
The launcher, Ask, the commands bar, the strip's search and the settings
fields are mono now, so what you typed is told from what the app says
at a glance. One ladder for rounding, each rung the one above over phi
squared, and every corner in the app sits on a rung.

The clipboard's addresses are drawn as the keys they are: the same cap
on every card and every pin that the guides and the sheet draw. A card's
header is the pill's trailing wing. The settings window's pane numbers
and row letters are the same caps, its headers speak in the body voice
rather than in capitals, its choices are named as names, and its legend
is gone: a plain question mark shows the window's keys on the sheet, as
it does inside the strip and inside every lens, and escape takes the
sheet down before it closes anything.
