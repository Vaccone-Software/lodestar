The editor: a line under what reads wrong, in everything you write.

Lodestar now reads what you type, in any app, and draws one thin line
under a word that is misspelled or a phrase that does not scan. Nothing
else appears: no badge, no count, no panel beside the text. A field with
no lines has been read and is fine. Press lode ⇥ and every mark wears a
letter and its fix; the letter applies it, ⇧ and the letter keeps what
you wrote, ⌫ takes the last fix back. If your hand is on the mouse, rest
the pointer on a mark and it offers the same two answers.

It corrects what you meant to write and never what it thinks you should
have meant. Spelling and grammar, never rewording, never tone, never a
name, never your lowercase or your casual words, never anything inside
code or a link. A word you keep becomes one of your words; a grammar
mark you keep is quiet in that sentence and teaches nothing, because the
same change is right in one sentence and wrong in the next.

A small language model reads each sentence when you finish it, on this
Mac and nowhere else. Choose how much it reads in Settings, on its own
page under 0: Spelling needs no model at all, Minimal uses Apple's own,
Standard is a 3.6 GB download that holds about 4 GB while you write, and
Full, for Macs with 64 GB, reads most precisely. The model loads when you
start typing and lets its memory go two minutes after you stop. Your
text never leaves the Mac and is never kept.

The editor is off until you turn it on, and when you do, it asks first.

This version needs a Mac with Apple silicon and macOS 14 or later. A
Mac that cannot run it stays on 0.36.3, and says so once.
