Every menu in Settings chooses again. Since 0.53, a click on a choice landed on the line's drawing and went no further, so nothing in a menu could be changed: not your sex, not the lode key, not the dictation model. A click anywhere on the line now chooses it, and so does return. Each menu in every place is now clicked through, choice by choice, before a release can ship.

Exact keyboard is no longer a setting. While health is on, each press is charged to the keyboard that sent it wherever macOS allows, which needs Input Monitoring. Lodestar asks for it once, and Settings shows it beside the other permissions under Health. Without it, presses are charged as they were before.
