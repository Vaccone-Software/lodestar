Every press walks first.

0.29.2 taught the verified copy's drag to move the pointer before it
presses, because Ghostty takes a press with no position. The click
door makes the same press: its fallback click on a word the tree
could not name, and its right-click. Both walk now, and all three
scripts come from one place, so the rule is decided once and a test
reads it. The events are stamped as Lodestar's own, as the drag's
already were, so the tap passes them to the app untouched and the
mouse record never counts them as the hand's.

The click door's harvest learned what select's learned in the
browser. Role, position, size, and children come back in one round
trip instead of four, and a container that lies off the window is
skipped with its whole subtree, so a heavy page spends the harvest's
deadline on the chips that can be seen rather than on the scrolled
away content Chromium keeps in its tree. The log line says batched
when the build that does this is the one running.

The probe learned the click door's bench: probe pressables walks an
app's window the way the harvest does, without touching focus, and
reports the node count, the time, and which elements press outside
the role list the harvest reads, so the next question about chips is
answered by a number rather than a guess.
