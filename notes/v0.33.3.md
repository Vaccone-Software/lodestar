Every surface the same shade, and a click that is only a click.

The launcher and the web bar read darker than the draft and the pill.
Photographed on one stage they were the same glass; on a desktop they
sat over different things, and the pill, being small and mostly edge,
ran a few levels lighter than a bar over the same ground. The veil
inside the glass now takes nearly all of the backdrop's vote: the bar,
the draft and the pill read 25 over charcoal and 29, 29 and 31 over a
white page, within a hand of levels of one another anywhere on the
screen. A trace of the frost remains at the rim.

A pick in the click door used to press and release faster than the
cursor could get there, so some apps took the press where the pointer
had last rested and the release at the target, and dragged a selection
between the two. The cursor is now placed first and the press waits for
it, so a click is a click.
