Lodestar puts a window where you are, and "where you are" is the display under the pointer. With two displays that broke the moment you crossed between them with the keyboard. Summon a window that lives on the other display, or throw the one in front there with lode ], and you were working on that screen while the pointer stayed on the first, so the next summon, the launcher and Keep all opened on the screen you had just left.

Now the pointer comes with you. Whenever Lodestar brings a window forward on a display the pointer is not on, by a summon, a throw, an undo or a breath, the pointer moves to the middle of that window. On one display it never moves, because the pointer there is yours.

A breath now remembers each display. Every window in it keeps the monitor it stood on, known by the monitor itself rather than by where it sat, and every display keeps its own direction, so a breath across a laptop and a monitor comes back on the right screens, side by side on one and stacked on the other, even after the monitors are rearranged. A window whose monitor is not attached joins the display you are on. Breaths saved before this release come back as they always did.

A tall display stacks. A monitor turned on end now divides top to bottom the first time windows share it, instead of into columns too narrow to read, and a display turned on its side follows, unless you had already flipped it the other way.
