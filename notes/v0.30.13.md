The switch slides.

The settings' switches, Lodestar's own since 0.30.12, arrived in place
without moving. The knob now crosses the track and the track's colour
crosses with it, in a fifth of a second; when a person has asked the
system for less motion, both simply arrive, as before.

The accent menu's swatch stood hard against its name and read as a
bullet. The dot now carries clear room after it.
