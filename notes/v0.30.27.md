Three corrections to the pill, from the first day with it.

The pill shows only what is being searched for. A capital is a pick,
not a search, and it belongs on the chips it narrows, not in the text
between the wings; it no longer appears there.

The sheet inside a lens answers a plain question mark. Holding the mode
key for it was one key too many, and a search was never made of a
question mark, so the lens gives that character to the sheet. Escape
still takes the sheet down with the lens up, and lode ? still works.

The typed text stands upright. The italic was tried on the glass and
did not look right, so the hand's words are set apart by size and
weight alone.
