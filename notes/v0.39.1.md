Feedback now reaches a person.

Report an Issue sent you to GitHub, where most people have no account
and the rest must write in public. In its place is Send Feedback, in
the menu under the star: a note written in Lodestar's own window that
goes straight to the person who makes it.

Nothing about you travels with it unless you add it. An email address
is there if you would like a reply, and left empty the note arrives all
the same. A diagnostic report can go with it, off until you tick it,
and you can read the report before you send, because its recent log
lines can name windows you had open.

If the note cannot be sent, it stays in the window and is also put on
the clipboard, so nothing you wrote is lost.
