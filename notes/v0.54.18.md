A release of fixes, ten of them, found by going through how Lodestar keeps track of what it is in the middle of.

Going straight from one lens to another, such as `lode /` with hints up, could leave the new one dead, with nothing on screen and your keys swallowed until esc. It now starts whole. Opening Keep with ⇧⌘V from scroll, select, hints or a chain now ends that mode first, where its pill, overlay or guide used to stay behind after Keep closed. What you type while the editor's lens lands a fix now reaches the app even if you press esc or a lode gesture before it lands.

A memory warning that arrives while the editor's model is loading now lets the weights go when the load ends, where they used to stay for up to twenty minutes. While a warning keeps the model away, nothing brings it back: starting dictation and the draft's own editor used to reload it. A question asked just after you change the editor's model is now answered by the model you chose.

Words from a crashed draft that could not be unsealed at launch are now kept and given back once the keychain opens. Closing any draft used to delete them. A keychain slow to answer at launch can no longer replace your coach history with only the last few seconds of events, and events from the first moments after launch now stay in that history.

In Settings, pressing ⏎ after a search landing and a click elsewhere could flip an unrelated setting or crash Lodestar. One `lode ←` could undo two gestures when a chord's letter had changed nothing. Closing a draft and opening the next while a Bluetooth headset was still waking could leave the new draft deaf. A rollback to the previous version now always says so, where it could go unannounced.
