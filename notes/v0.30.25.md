The scroll guide is a band, and the map waits for a hesitation.

Scroll mode used to open a six-row panel over the pane being read, every
time, including the thousandth. Now its resting state is one line along
the bottom edge, in the same place and the same shape as select's band:
the mode, the app, and the word the aim landed on, with the keys named
quietly after them. The two lenses are siblings, and now they look it;
opening the aim is the band changing its verb rather than one panel
giving way to another.

The map of keys still exists, above the band, but it waits. The same
fade the bars' footers earn governs it, on the scroll verb's own count:
a new hand gets the rows at once, a hand that has used the mode thirty
times gets them two seconds after it hesitates, and a hand that knows
its keys never reads them at all. An escape with nothing done in the
mode is the stumble that brings the rows straight back for an hour. A
map that always paints always gets read, and reading is reconstruction,
not recall.

The rows themselves read plainer: one separator where there were three
widths of dot, and the aim row says what it does in the voice of the
others.
