The glass is charcoal again.

This morning's release tinted every panel toward black, and every panel
arrived a shade darker than it was drawn: grey 6 where the design's
charcoal is 26. The material does not blend toward a colour the way a
veil does; it multiplies with what is behind it, so aiming at black, or
at charcoal itself, both land near black. The tint now aims at a lighter
grey chosen by photograph so that the panel lands on its own ground: 25
over charcoal, 65 over a white page at nearly eight to one, and in light
mode paper over anything. Reduce Transparency draws an opaque veil in the
ground's colour, since a tint cannot be made opaque either.

Nothing else changed.
