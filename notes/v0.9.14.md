Marks retire, and the graph keeps the addresses.

Marks gave a letter to one specific window. The graph outgrew them.
It reaches apps and browser profiles, it nests as deep as you like, so
the things you open twice a week live one letter further in rather
than in a second namespace to remember. The window chooser reaches the
other windows of whatever you are in. Breaths hold whole saved worlds.
What was left for a mark to do never justified the key it held.

There was a flaw worth naming on the way out. A mark pinned a live
window, so it died whenever that window closed, which made it exactly
the wrong tool for the occasional app it was meant to serve. Any
future address for a specific thing has to survive the thing being
gone, the way a breath relaunches what it needs.

Nothing to do on your side. A saved mark in your state file is read
past quietly and written away on the next save, and the backtick it
lived on now reaches your app untouched.

The guides, the cheat sheet, the documentation, and the site all speak
lode throughout.
