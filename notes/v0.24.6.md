An address you accept now replaces the one it was offered against.

Until now, saying yes to a shorter address bound the new one and quietly
left the old one working. That sounds harmless and is not. The coach
exists to make your hand faster, and it cannot do that while the habit it
argued against is still there to fall back on. The research on exactly
this is unambiguous: when both paths stay open, most people never make
the switch at all, and it is not for want of knowing the new one.

So an accepted suggestion is now a replacement. The new address is bound
and the old one is removed, in a single write. Nothing is kept in your
config to mark it, because a config should hold what you wrote and
nothing else. The coach already remembers what it said and what you
answered, and that turns out to be all the memory this needs.

Press the old address afterwards and it tells you where it went rather
than reporting itself missing. That is the whole of the nicety, and it is
deliberately small: the flash is the same one any unbound address gets,
with different words. What teaches your hand is that the old way stops
working, not anything written on the screen. What the message is for is
that a change you asked for should never look like something breaking.

This is not only about length. A replacement of the same length can win
by no longer fighting the hand you already have, and when that suggestion
arrives it will move an address the same way.

If you want an address back, the launcher has always had it. ⌘K on any
app opens its bindings and you can put it wherever you like.
