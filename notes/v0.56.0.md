Reduce loud audio keeps headphone volume at a safe level.

In Settings, General, Sound and display, choose Light, Standard or Strong. Standard is safe for long listening every day, Strong is for children or the most protection, and Light suits noisy places. The volume keys stop at the limit, and the slider or a headset's buttons are brought back to it. The first time it stops the volume, a note says why and where it is set.

It is off until you choose a level. Only headphones are limited, Bluetooth and wired, never speakers, and nothing is recorded or measured.
