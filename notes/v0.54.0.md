Health now keeps the whole of what it can measure, and the facts you give it about yourself are sealed with it.

About you has four rows: the year you were born, your sex as assigned at birth, the hand you write with, and whether you touch type. They live beside the health record, under its key, and go wherever it goes: exported with it, deleted with it. They are no longer in the config file. If yours held a year or a hand, this release moves them into the sealed record the first time it opens and takes them out of the file, and the config's history no longer keeps them, including what an earlier release wrote there. The Health setting now says the finger readings assume touch typing, because they do.

When you dictate, health now measures your voice as the draft closes: its pitch and how much it moves, how loud it is, how steady it is from one cycle to the next, how clear it is, where you paused, and how fast you spoke. Only those numbers are kept. The audio is dropped as it always was. Each measurement notes what kind of microphone heard you, never its name. `lodestar observations` shows a voice line once there is something to show.

The pointer record no longer throws motion away. A movement that ended in a click was always kept. Now so is one that came to rest without a click, and one made with a button held down. Each scroll keeps its own movement too, with the part your fingers made kept apart from the glide that followed.

Health also notes what kind of app you were in, such as a browser, a terminal or a chat, beside the app's name, so a record can be read the same way across people who use different apps for the same work.
