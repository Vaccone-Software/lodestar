The walkthrough, rebuilt around the tools rather than around questions.

Nine cards, each named for the thing it teaches: Accessibility, Searcher,
Graph, Breaths, Inside a window, Action menu, Clipboard, Web bar, and a
closing note. Somebody who knows what the graph is can work out when to
reach for it. Somebody told only about outcomes has to be taught every
one of them separately.

A lesson is now the real panel. Press the gesture a card is teaching and
the walkthrough steps out of the way: the actual searcher, the actual hint
overlay, the actual clipboard strip runs over your own windows, and the
card returns when the screen is quiet again. The previous version drew its
own versions of those panels inside the card, which is how it managed to
teach the searcher without ever showing one and to draw hint labels that
did not match the real ones. Nothing here is a picture of a panel now, so
nothing here can fall out of date.

The permission card always appears, whether or not the grant is already in
place, and it is where Lodestar asks what to call you. Used sparingly,
kept on the machine.

Backspace goes back, which is where a hand reaches for it. Return always
continues, so a lesson that fails to register cannot strand anybody.

Smaller things, all of them from looking at the screens rather than
reasoning about them: the card is the same glass as every other surface at
the same corner radius, one height across the deck so paging no longer
resizes the panel under the reader, spacing that varies by relationship
instead of one number everywhere, a narrower measure for the text, your
own accent colour on the progress and the state lines, and a name field
that looks like a field.
