One glass reads the same on every surface.

The draft looked different from the launcher, and the photographs said
it was not: on a flat ground the two measured to the level. The
difference was the backdrop. The glass lets a share of what is behind it
through, and a small bar sits over one thing while the draft, being
large, sits over several, so a bright window behind half of it read as
a patch. No tint colour or strength removes that share; every one tried
moved the panel darker or lighter as a whole and left the patch alone.
What removes it is a veil in the ground's own colour inside the glass,
taking most of the backdrop's vote and leaving the frost the rest. The
draft now reads the same over a white page as over a dark one, within
twelve levels, and so does everything else, because every surface is the
one glass.

Nothing else changed.
