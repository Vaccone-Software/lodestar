The clipboard history keeps its state on one thread, and now it says so. Reading a card's files, checking whether they are still where they were, and deleting from disk all happen on other threads, and every answer comes back to the main thread before it touches what the strip draws from. That was always how it worked, but nothing held the code to it. The places where those answers land, and the calls that delete cards or ask after their files, now stop at once if they are ever reached from anywhere else, so a mistake would show up in the tests long before it could quietly corrupt the history.

Nothing you see changes. A suspected race in how the strip remembers which files are still present was looked into and turned out not to exist. This release makes sure it cannot appear later.
