⌃J and ⌃K walk the list now, next to the keys that already did.

The launcher, the web bar and menu search answer four pairs of keys for
moving the highlight: the arrows, ⌃N and ⌃P, and now ⌃J and ⌃K. Two of
those were quietly there from the beginning — macOS binds ⌃N and ⌃P to the
same commands the arrow keys send, and these panels have answered those
commands since they were written — so a hand that learned its habits in a
terminal has always been at home here without anything saying so. ⌃J and ⌃K
are for the hands that learned somewhere else.

One habit changes to make room. Inside those three search fields, ⌃K stops
killing to the end of the line and moves the highlight up instead. Nothing
outside them is touched: the fields are one line long, ⌫, ⌥⌫ and ⌘⌫ still do
what they always did, and the tail end of a five-character query was never
worth a keystroke. The clipboard strip is deliberately left out — its cards
are addressed by the home row, where J and K are already two of the
addresses, and a key that means two things is worse than a key that means
one.
