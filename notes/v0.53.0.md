Lodestar has a new look: matte ceramic. Every surface is one opaque pane lit from above, what you can press stands on a visible side, and a place you type into sinks. The glass is gone, and with it the tint that drifted with whatever lay behind it.

There is one colour, International Orange, set for each look: deeper by day, brighter at night, so it reads the same on both. The accent setting is gone. A config that still sets it loads without complaint, and the key is never written back.

The type is one family on four sizes and three weights, one weight heavier at night. Mono stays only where vim is, in the draft and the editor's lens.

Eye comfort is on by default. Through the evening every surface dims toward the night, a little more at bedtime, and never takes on a tint; the Mac still decides light or dark. The pill and the meeting's chip recede after twenty seconds alone and come back at a hover or a key. It is one switch, in Sound and display.

Every key is drawn one way, as you type it. A sequence is one key per keystroke, a double tap is the key and ×2, and a comma or a period sits low on its key as it does on a real keycap. Keys are centred as whole objects, so they no longer look low beside their words.

The chosen row, card or tile is a slab in the other look's colours, and only the key that acts on it is lit. In Keep, the whole chosen card is the slab.

Keep no longer flashes Shift's labels as it opens. A modifier still down from ⇧⌘V does not count, neither on the cards nor for what ⏎ does, until you let go of it and press it again.

A field has one caret and one selection everywhere. The field you are typing in shows it with a deeper edge, not a ring.

Lists keep one rhythm: rows, settings, lines and legends each have one height and one way of laying out. A setting that cannot act is drawn quiet, its value still showing, instead of faded. A setting's letter is a key, and the letters follow the page's reading order, so some have moved. Both Model menus now read just Automatic.

The coach's offer shows its whole record, wrapping onto a second line when it needs one, and says how often you do a thing in plain words: "6 times more than chance".

The meeting's chip has three moods. Before the meeting it is plain. From the last minute to 90 seconds in, its stub is orange. After that it steps back: the stub plain, "MIN AGO", the whole chip receded, and `lode` `lode` no longer lit, though it still joins. Typing does not wake it, since you may be typing in the meeting; a hover does.

Nothing slides or fades now except the voice meter and a switch's knob. A change of state happens at once.
