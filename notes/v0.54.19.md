A release of foundations: Lodestar now runs on Stoic 0.4.2.

A model or update download that sends more than its server declared now stops at once, where it used to keep writing into the file.

A config number that must be whole, has a fraction and is out of range, such as `"max-size-mb": 99999.5`, now falls back to its default and says so, where it used to become the nearest bound.

A mark you fix or change while the editor is reading the field is now recorded for what you did, where it could be counted as changed even when you had fixed it, and with too long a time standing.
