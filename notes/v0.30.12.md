The switches wear the accent.

macOS's switch takes its tint from the system accent and offers no way
to say otherwise, so a person who chose International Orange saw the
settings' switches in the Mac's colour. The settings' switches are now
Lodestar's own control: the same gesture, click or space or a screen
reader's press, drawn by the theme. And the accent's menu wears a
swatch of each colour beside its name, so the two are compared by eye
where they are chosen. Only the menus' highlight and the focus rings
on text fields keep the system's tint.
