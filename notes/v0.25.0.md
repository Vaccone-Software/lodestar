Lodestar remembers a year, coaches with its hands instead of its mouth,
and stops dropping keystrokes it was handed in good faith.

**A year of memory.** The observation ring kept ninety days, which was
enough for every model and not enough for a mirror: you cannot show
someone their spring if it fell off the tape in July. The raw log now
keeps a full year, and the trick that makes that free is that it is
sharded by month — a closed month is written once and never parsed again,
retention retires whole files instead of rewriting anything, and the
recommendation pass still reads only the ninety days its evidence lives
in. Boot pays what it always paid. The archive just stops forgetting.

**The hands join the record.** With `observations.health` — its own
switch, on by default, off in one line — Lodestar keeps a pulse of the
hands themselves: keystrokes and clicks counted, scrolling counted as
reaches rather than wheel events, active minutes, the rhythm between
keys, and the backspace rate, which is the correction signal strain
research watches. One line is structural and permanent: never key
identities on general typing. Which keys, which apps, which words —
none of it is kept, because timing individual keys on ordinary text is
a transcript wearing a lab coat. What is kept is counts and moments,
and nothing anywhere judges them. `lodestar observations` shows the
mirror; the quarterly retrospective will hold it properly.

**The instrument watches itself.** The same log that times your pauses
now times Lodestar's own: how long scroll mode took to find its panes,
how long a retile held the AX line. A regression in the instrument
surfaces exactly the way a regression in the hand does, in the same
report, priced in the same seconds.

**The coach gains two hands.** The evidence has been on the wall since
CHI 2007: closing an old path moves hands, and advice mostly does not.
So the coach's new powers reshape rather than say. The first reads your
errors as proposals — the wrong letter you keep pressing names the
address your head believes in, the place you go in the seconds after
names the destination you wanted, and when those recoveries agree the
chip offers to bind the destination at the address you already press,
retiring the old one behind the same expiring redirect a supersede uses.
The second is for the pair of apps you bounce between hundreds of times
a week: accepting arranges them side by side on the spot and saves the
layout as a breath at a free letter, because once an address is fast the
only gain left is the navigation that stops existing. It composes only
what is already open — an accept never launches anything behind you.

And the curriculum now keeps score on itself: each kind of suggestion is
weighted by what its past accepts actually did — a habit that took is a
win, a "never" is a loss, an accept still learning is neither — so the
coach leads with the interventions that demonstrably work on you.
Evidence still decides what may be offered. History decides what goes
first.

**The map earns its silence.** The chain guide has always painted the
moment a chain starts, and for a fresh address that is right. But a map
that always appears always gets read, and reading is not recall — recall
is what compiles the habit. The guide now waits longer to paint as every
address under the prefix bends, up to two seconds over a subtree you
fully own, and one stumble — a wrong key, an abandoned chain — brings it
straight back for an hour. A fluent chain never sees it. A stalled one
still gets it, one honest beat later. `guide.fade false` pins the old
behavior.

**Nine windows, and a refusal that means it.** The digits are the
addresses, so nine is the cap: a tenth window made `lode 9` promise a
"last" no digit could reach. The tenth beside-summon used to quietly
take over the display, which is the one thing "beside" must never mean;
now it is refused with the reason and the world untouched.

**No keystroke lands in the wrong register.** Entering scroll mode on a
large, cold window could take seconds to find the pane, and the j you
had already committed fell through to the graph as a miss. The mode now
enters instantly against whatever is in front — wheel events land under
the pointer regardless — and the pane discovery catches up behind it.
In the same spirit, the retile's conversation with other apps moved off
the keyboard's thread entirely: a wedged app can now stall a window
arrangement, and nothing else. The keyboard is never its hostage.
