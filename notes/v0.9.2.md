The deliberate half of the bargain.

Since 0.9.1, windows you did not summon float untouched. This release adds
the other half: `hyper =` claims the focused window, giving it the summon
treatment on demand. It becomes the active display's layout, full screen,
everything else parked. `hyper ⇧=` (the + key) adds it beside the current
layout instead. Equals to become the world, plus to join it.

A claim is a summon: silent on success, undoable with `hyper Z`, and doing
nothing when the window already owns the layout.

Also in this release: the = and - keys join the keycode table, and
`app.disabled-gestures` accepts "=".
