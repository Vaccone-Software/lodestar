Settings is simpler. Where a choice only asked you to decide something Lodestar can decide well, it now decides, and the places are nine instead of ten.

These are no longer settings. Scrolling is always smooth, and Scroll speed is the one scroll setting. The walk is due after thirty minutes. A meeting's chip appears five minutes before it starts. Windows and panels land on the display under the pointer. Eye comfort is always on. The editor spells the way your Mac's language and region do. A finished selection is always copied. A copied timestamp reads into your zone and UTC. A config file that still names any of these keeps working.

The editor and the draft choose their models for your Mac. Settings no longer lists them. Instead, each switch says in one line what runs on this Mac and how much it downloads. After your first dictation, the draft asks once before it downloads its models, as the editor does when you turn it on. Decline, and the draft turns off. If you have dictated before, nothing is asked.

Web and Meetings are now Routing, since a meeting link is routed like any other link. Observations is now Wellbeing, and Reduce loud audio has moved there, next to the walk. Input Monitoring is listed once, in Input.

Switch lists your letters and the app each one opens, so you can remove one and free its letter for another app, or add one. Save images to opens the Mac's folder picker instead of asking you to type a path. Ask offers Startpage as a search engine. Some rows have clearer names: Update channel, Input device, Rules, and Names and terms.
