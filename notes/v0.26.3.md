Ask the player, not the air.

0.26.2 opened the gate and the pause finally fired, and the measurement
that followed killed the mechanism underneath it. The design inferred
"something is playing" from CoreAudio's process roll call and pressed
the Now Playing ⏯. Two facts of this machine broke both halves: a
paused player keeps its silent output stream open for fifteen seconds,
so the check that something had stopped read "no effect" and stranded
the pause; and a keyboard-click app holds a stream open forever, so
silence read as music, and a blind toggle pointed at silence starts
music instead of stopping it.

Now the draft asks the players themselves, by Apple Events: Music and
Spotify answer "playing" or they do not, pause means pause, play means
play, and only a player that said "playing" is ever owed a resume. A
hand that presses play first settles the debt. A browser tab cannot be
asked and is left alone. The first pause will ask you once to let
Lodestar control Music, and never again.

Everything else stands: the shared-radio gate, the resume that waits
for the headset to speak music again, the three-second backstop, no
setting anywhere.
