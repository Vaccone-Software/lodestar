The shadow follows the glass.

Every key panel — the searcher, the web bar, menu search — wore a faint
rectangle: a hairline traced around the window's raw rect, square corners
floating over the rounded glass. On macOS 26 the window server derives a
key window's shadow from the window's shape, and a borderless, clear
window has no shape to offer — so the whole shadow package, hairline
included, fell back to the frame rectangle. Panels that never take key
focus — the HUD, the cheat sheet, hints — were always drawn from their
content, which is why only the bars were haunted.

The bars are now titled windows in borderless clothes: hidden title,
transparent chrome, content filling the full frame. Geometry is unchanged
to the pixel, but the window server finally holds a real rounded shape,
and the hairline hugs the glass edge the way it does on every native
pane.

Bars also re-derive their shadow after every reframe, so a searcher that
grows and shrinks under fast typing can never wear a stale silhouette.
