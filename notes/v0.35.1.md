Lodestar froze on the first click.

0.35.0's mouse tap took its lock for the whole of a click, then asked
whether the lid was closed under the same lock, which does not
re-enter. The tap's thread waited on itself; the next keystroke waited
on the tap; the system switched the key tap off for not answering, and
the right command key fell through as plain command. If that happened
to you: sorry. Quit and relaunch, and this build does not do it.

The lid now has a lock of its own, a test proves that a click and a
scroll both come back on a thread of their own, and the raw stores are
placed beside the observations rather than at a fixed path, so that
test could not touch a real record.
