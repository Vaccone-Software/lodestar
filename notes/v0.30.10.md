The accent follows you everywhere.

Choosing International Orange changed the cursor and the lit letters
and left the launcher's highlight, the settings' dots, the walk's star
and the match washes in the Mac's own colour. Every surface Lodestar
draws now takes the accent from one place, and a test reads the
sources to prove nothing reaches for the system's colour directly, so
the choice cannot drift again. The one tint it does not reach is the
one macOS paints on its own native controls in the settings window.

The choices are named as names: System, and International Orange.
