A new mark.

The compass rose that stood for Lodestar did its job, and read as a
compass: the thing you take a bearing with, not the star you steer by.
In its place is a star with depth, twelve points drawn as flat planes of
International Orange, each lit only by the way it faces, on a charcoal
that carries a little of the orange. It is in the Dock, in Finder, on
the disk image, and in the menu bar, where its silhouette stands at
rest and knocks out of a disc while a chain is pending, as the rose did.

The star is defined once and every copy is drawn from that definition,
in whatever color it is asked for, so it can follow wherever color on
the Mac goes next.
