The finds light up.

0.26.5 taught `f`, `t`, `F`, and `T` to cross line breaks and promised
an accent-colored answer in the text. The answer never appeared: it was
drawn as a wash behind the letters, and the glass's vibrancy composited
it into nothing. The state was right, the tests of the state passed,
and the screen showed none of it — so there is now a test that reads
the rendered storage itself, the attributes the screen reads, and it
would have failed on that build.

The lights also come earlier now, the way quickdraw does it: press `f`
and the first instance of each character you could reach lights in
your accent color, the letters themselves recolored and weighted, so
you see where every key would land before you commit to one.
Whitespace is jumpable but never painted. The character you name stays
lit for `;` and `,` until the next navigation or edit.
