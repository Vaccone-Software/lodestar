Breaths offered where they are wanted, and a draft that remembers what it heard.

The coach had been offering breaths as its default. Ten of its last
twelve suggestions were layouts, and not one of the four you accepted
was ever brought back. The reason was in what it counted: two apps used
one after the other, all day, read as two apps that belong together.
They do not. Ghostty and Brave, the pair it pressed hardest, were each
pulled into view on its own nearly every time the week after their
breath was saved. The hand wanted one window at a time, and the chord
already composes the rare pair it wants together.

So a breath is now offered only for a pair you stand side by side
yourself, with a shift summon, a chord or a beside move, about twice a
week, and it is priced on those arrangements, because a breath replaces
composing, not switching. And an accepted breath is judged the way an
address is: by coming back. It holds the coach's one learning slot until
you have recalled it a handful of times, at any letter and in any layout
that still holds the pair, and one that is never recalled counts against
the kind, where the accept alone used to count as a success. When
things are already quick, the coach now has less to say, which is
right.

The draft also keeps something it used to throw away. When a draft
ends with nothing in it, the record now holds how loud the microphone
was: a device delivering nothing at all, a quiet room, or a voice. A
draft closed in a second and a draft that waited on a deaf microphone
look the same from the outside, and only this can tell them apart.
