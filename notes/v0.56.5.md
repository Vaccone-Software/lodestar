When two or more windows stand side by side because Lodestar placed them there, the window your keys go to now wears a faint line in the accent, just inside its edge. It shows only while that window is where the layout left it. Drag or resize it, or let another app's window cover it, and the line goes away until a layout key places the window again. A single window, or one you opened and arranged yourself, never wears it. There is no setting for it.

Dictation's models now load and let go in the order they were asked. A release that arrived during a load could leave a model in memory, and a late one could unload the model just loaded and lose the next phrase.

A config edit that fails now says plainly what happened and what to do. Music and Spotify get two seconds to answer, so a player that hangs no longer holds up playback, and a question typed into Settings gives up after five seconds and shows the matching rows. A saved clipboard image, the editor's schema, the alert sound and the login agent are written so that a failed save leaves the file that was already there.
