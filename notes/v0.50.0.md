Lodestar now keeps your clipboard history and health record encrypted on disk, its clipboard is faster, and feedback is never lost.

Your clipboard history, your keystroke and pointer health record, and the
logbook are now sealed at rest with keys kept in your login keychain. What
is already on disk is encrypted in the background on first launch, each
file checked before the old copy is removed. Deleting a clip now really
deletes it: within seconds its key is gone, so nothing on the disk can be
read back. If the keychain cannot be opened, Lodestar pauses that history
rather than touching it, and never deletes anything on its own.
`lodestar health export <folder>` writes your health record out in the old
plain formats for your own scripts. GUIDE.md says what is sealed, what is
not, and what that does and does not protect against.

The clipboard is faster. A large copy costs the main thread milliseconds
instead of most of a second, and loading the history no longer holds up
launch. Copying something you copied before just brings its card back to
the front. Thumbnails are decoded off the main thread.

Feedback is never lost. A note is saved before it is sent, and if you are
offline Lodestar keeps trying on its own, even after a quit. The site
sends each note once, however often it arrives.

The config file is read and written by Stoic's settings layer. A mistake in
`lodestar.json` is now reported with its line and column, and Lodestar keeps
your last working config instead of falling back to the defaults. A key
written twice is read with the last value and flagged, and comments are
refused before a rewrite could lose them.

Editor models and updates download through Stoic. Each file is checked as
it streams, a download resumes where it stopped, and an update is checked
against the digest GitHub publishes, with no three-minute cap on a slow
connection.
