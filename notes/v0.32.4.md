The glass stops changing with the page behind it.

Every Lodestar surface is Liquid Glass, and glass shows what is behind
it. Over a dark window that was fine. Over a white page the dark bar
turned to a mid grey and its text thinned to below the floor Lodestar
sets for anything read, and the pill, the smallest surface, fared worst.
A veil inside the glass had been correcting the tone after the fact. It
held the tone. It did not hold the weight.

macOS 27 added a slider for how clear Liquid Glass should be, and
photographing Lodestar at every position of it showed that the clearest
setting, which is the default, was the one that read worst. The fix is
not the slider. The glass is now tinted toward the system's own ground
through the material itself, black on charcoal and white on paper, and
the veil is gone. Over a white page the bar sits sixteen levels from its
charcoal self and its text reads above twelve to one; the pill goes from
three and a half to twelve and a half. The frost and the rim are as they
were. The slider is left to you, and at its clearest Lodestar now clears
its own floors.

Nothing else changed.
