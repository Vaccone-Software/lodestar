Lodestar has a voice, and the coach speaks in it.

The coach's chip used to put its offer on a key row and its answers in
a footer. Now it speaks: the offer as a sentence in the serif, Slack
could be one key away, with the keymap drawn as the keys it is beneath
it and the measurements beside them in the interface's face. Forty-one
searches, about ninety seconds a week, about an hour a year: the same
number twice, once as measured and once at a scale a person can feel.
The two answers are named as the verbs they are, Accept and Decline. A
decline is answered in the same voice, once, and then the glass is
clear; the offer sleeps a season.

The voice is New York, the system's own serif, and it is reserved for
sentences Lodestar says when it asks, teaches or reflects. It never says
I. It says you. It names Lodestar only where Lodestar is the subject, so
an update on its way says so and the start of a session says only that
it is ready when you are, with the way in drawn as the keys it is. A
fact stays in the interface's face, so the two speakers are never
confused, and no title ends with a period.

Every flash is now one family with the pill. The glyph a flash opened
with named its kind; the kind draws as a symbol in the pill's own
configuration, and the line opens with a capital: refused, needs you,
the clipboard, opening, done, the layout. Breaths wear wind, because a
breath is taken and released, not filed. A breath saved says so in a
sentence, with the windows it holds and the apps it brings back named
the way a sentence names them.
