The coach arrives.

Lodestar has watched how you reach things since the last release; now it can
say something about it. When the observations hold a finding that clears the
gates, a small glass chip appears after a navigation: one suggestion, priced
in your own numbers, at a moment you can verify the claim from your own last
few seconds. "you searched for it 31 times this month · about 40 seconds a
week".

Its whole grammar lives on the lode key. Tap lode twice, the keycap on the
chip says so, and the coach writes exactly one config line through the same
path ⌘K uses, flashing the receipt. Lode ⌫ means not this one: the
suggestion sleeps for a season and returns only if the evidence keeps
growing. Ignoring it is also an answer. The chip fades on its own, parks in
the menu bar as an inbox of at most one, and may try once or twice more at
a better moment before parking quietly. A live camera silences the coach
entirely; it never speaks into a meeting.

It paces itself by what your hands demonstrate rather than by a calendar.
After you accept a new address the coach stays silent until that address has
actually compiled into your fingers, then considers the next. Expect a
suggestion or two a month at most, and silence for the first weeks while the
evidence accrues. Anything that cannot be committed as one config line never
reaches the chip; it stays in `lodestar observations`, where the whole
ledger of what the coach has said, and what became of it, is always
readable.

The coach is a register like the launcher or the graph, not a person: no
greetings, no praise, no first person. One line, with its numbers attached.
`coach.enabled` set to false turns the voice off; the findings remain in the
report either way.
