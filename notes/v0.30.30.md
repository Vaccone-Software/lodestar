one ready instead of two, and a green check on every push.

minimum width and left empty glass to the right of short words. A flash
is now exactly as wide as what it says.

to launchd, and launchd's own instance said ready again. The relay now
holds its tongue: only the instance that stays announces itself.

and failed on the continuous-integration runner's small screen after
