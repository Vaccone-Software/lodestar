Lodestar's pictures and symbols are drawn anew, as one set. Each is a few of Lodestar's own things — the Mac with the star on its screen, a page with one word marked, windows wearing a hint key, the copied cards, the pill listening, a key and the pointer, a link sent to its window, the keyboard's two halves, a sprout — made as rounded slabs seen a little from above and engraved like a print: a face in the key's colour, the side it stands on cut with fine lines, and orange only where your hand goes. The symbols in the pill, Keep and Settings are the same things drawn small, so the bin, the pin and the folder you meet in a menu are the ones in the pictures. At the size text wears the engraving would be finer than a pixel, so a small symbol keeps its edges and its shade and lets the lines go.

Settings gives its nine places more room, and each is the same size. The place under the pointer rises onto the slab — the one motion Settings has, over in a tenth of a second, and none at all with Reduce Motion on. The line under each name is shorter: Links and browsers, Keyboards and mice, Breaks and hearing. The tails of letters like g and y in those lines are no longer cut off.

Safari and Firefox profiles now work everywhere Brave's do: in your letters, in Ask, in rules and the fallback, in clicked links and in meetings. Write them as safari:Name and firefox:Name, or pick them from the same lists as Brave's. Safari's profiles are read from its File menu while it runs, so open Safari once and they appear. A link for a Safari profile opens as a new tab in that profile's window, or in a new window of it, and Safari stays behind the app you are in. Firefox's profiles are read once you turn on Firefox profiles in Settings, under Routing.

macOS never asks before it keeps another app's data from Lodestar. The first time Lodestar reads a browser's profiles, macOS adds a switch for that browser in Privacy & Security, under Files & Folders, and leaves it off. Until now a browser whose switch was off seemed to have no profiles at all. Routing now has a Browsers group that shows each browser's profiles, and when macOS keeps them from Lodestar it names the switch to turn on and opens the page. The rules and links that name that browser say the same. Turning the switch on works at once.

In the editor, ⏎ applies every fix and closes the lens, and the next key you press goes to the app again. Taking fixes back is the app's own ⌘Z. In most apps one ⌘Z takes back the whole set of fixes, and in the draft one ⌘Z, or u, does. The editor's own Undo pill is gone.
