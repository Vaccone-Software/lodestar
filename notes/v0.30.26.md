Every lens wears one pill, and the sheet knows which lens is up.

Scroll, its aim band, click and select each had a band of their own, in
its own shape, saying its own things. Now there is one object: a pill on
the glass, floating where the guide has always stood, that every lens
shares. Its left wing is the mode, a symbol and a word; its right wing is
the app, a name and its icon, told from the mode by tone alone. Between
them is what you have said. Nothing, while you have said nothing. A still
hairline caret when the lens is listening for your typing, so the band
says whether letters would land without carrying an instruction. And the
letters themselves, in italic, the moment you have typed or aimed at
anything, at which point both wings fold to their glyphs and the symbol
and the icon stay exactly where they were. Nothing moves and nothing
animates, because a mode indicator's one job is to be true the instant
the mode is true.

The pill carries no instruction, not even esc. The keys a lens owns are
on the sheet: lode ? inside scroll, the aim band, click or select shows
every key that lens answers to, and esc takes the sheet down with the
lens still up. Outside a lens, lode ? is the whole system as before.

Every dimension of the pill is its height over a power of φ, so one
number sets the object and the next surface inherits it. The scroll
guide's rows and their fade are gone with the band they stood on; the
sheet is where the map lives now, one key away, for as long as anyone
wants it.
