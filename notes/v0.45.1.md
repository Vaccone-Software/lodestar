The icon is redrawn in the same language as everything inside the app.

The star floats on its own soft shadow over a flat plate: clay by day,
the warm night by night, with a fine edge and no gloss. On macOS 26 the
icon in Finder and Launchpad changes with your appearance. While
Lodestar is running, its icon in the Dock, in ⌘⇥ and in its own windows
also wears your accent colour, or International Orange if you chose it,
and changes the moment you change either.

Three last places that still looked like the system now look like
Lodestar. A search in Settings lands on its row by raising it, the way
a bar raises the row you are about to take, instead of with a glowing
ring. Uninstall opens in a window of Lodestar's own: the whole plan, a
switch for also removing everything Lodestar has kept, and its keys,
esc to cancel and ⌘⏎ to uninstall. And nothing is painted red any more;
what cannot be undone says so in its words and sits below a line.
