An image opens large, and is saved by name.

An image card in the clipboard strip had one verb, and it wrote a
stamped file to Downloads without asking. Now E on the card opens the
image above the strip, the way a text card opens in the draft: the card
lit beneath, the pin column aside, the image as large as the display
allows without inventing a pixel. Escape or return steps back. S, on the
card or inside its door, turns the strip's band into a name for the
file, typed the way the search is and never key. The band offers a name
before anything is typed, where the image came from and when, so return
alone is enough; a slash in the name is a subfolder, a leading slash or
tilde is a path of its own, and .jpg or .tiff chooses the format. The
folder is clipboard.save-to, Downloads unless you say otherwise, in
Settings under Clipboard.
