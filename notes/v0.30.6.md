The pins have their own place.

The pin column once climbed from the same corner as the recents, so a
pin and the newest copy were one hot region. The record says that
region was not hot, two pins in a year of history and not one paste
from a pin, and a column pressed against the row read as an extension
of it. It now stands centered on the screen's left edge, its own zone,
still drawn with one free slot showing, because that slot is how a
hand that has never pinned learns that it can. On a short screen the
column comes down no further than the row above the recents, where
the search band lives. The band takes the strip's full width when the
column is clear of it and starts to the column's right when it is not.

Small text has one look. Captions, legends, and flashes are three
grammars, and every one is set the same way: the meta size, regular
weight, the secondary colour. A card's foot is one caption now, how
long then how old, 12 lines then 3m ago, joined by a middle dot.

The mode bands speak. Hints and select used to name the window and
say type what you see in one mono status line at caption size. The
one thing the hand is asked to do is now set in the body voice and
the label colour, the facts around it in the caption voice, and the
typed letters still echoed in mono.
