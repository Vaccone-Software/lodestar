Lodestar now comes in two channels, and the one most Macs follow waits until a build has proven itself.

Every new build goes first to Preview. It reaches Stable on its own after
it has been out a while: a new version after seven days, a fix after
three, and never a build less than a day old. A fix arriving during the
wait never delays it, because when the newest is too young the one
before it goes instead. Stable is where every Mac starts. Settings has
the choice under Updates, Stable or Preview. A Mac that leaves Preview
for Stable stays where it is until Stable passes it, because Lodestar
never moves backwards.

Which build is stable is decided in one place, Lodestar's website, from
the public list of releases and the date. Every Mac, the download on the
site and the Homebrew cask ask the same question and get the same
answer, and the rule can be improved without every Mac updating first. A
Mac on Stable still takes only a zip from Lodestar's own releases,
signed by Lodestar's own developer, so the site can only choose among
builds that were already published. If it cannot be reached, nothing
changes that day.

This release itself reaches every Mac at once, because the version
before it knew only one channel. From here on yours follows Stable
unless you choose Preview.
