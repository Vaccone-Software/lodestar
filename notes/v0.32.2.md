The microphone stops going deaf after an update.

Updating Lodestar has been leaving dictation silent, on and off, since
0.25.0. You would open the draft, speak, and get nothing: no words, no
meter, no error. It usually came back after a few minutes, or after
quitting and reopening, which is why it read as flakiness rather than as
a bug with a shape.

It has a shape. An audio engine can start, report that it is running,
and deliver no sound at all, and nothing about it looks wrong from the
outside: the same microphone, the same format, no error. The minute
after an update is when one gets built that way. And because keeping a
warm engine is worth a second of startup, that deaf one was kept and
restarted for every draft that followed.

Now it is watched instead of trusted. An engine that claims to be
running and delivers nothing for a second and a half is rebuilt on the
spot, in the middle of the session, so you lose a moment rather than
the sentence. A session that hears nothing at all is not allowed to
hand its engine on to the next one.

This is also most of the reason three in ten dictations used to settle
no words.
