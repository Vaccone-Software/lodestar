Settings is easier to scan: Remove, Add, Reset and Undo are now small icon keys.

Every list entry ends in a minus, every add line in a plus, and a value you changed, or a change in History, can be put back with one arc. Hover over a key to see its name, and VoiceOver reads it. The keyboard works as before.

The launcher's field has a new icon of its own: a lens holding Lodestar's star, its handle one of the star's points.

A correction picked in the editor's lens after a short pause now lands where it should, where it could say the text had changed.
