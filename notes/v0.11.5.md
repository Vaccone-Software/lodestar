The walkthrough opens once, on a first run, and never again on its own.

It was keyed to the version, which made it a full screen modal on every
release. The updater applies while you are idle and the deck is suppressed for
that boot, so the launch after an update was the one that opened it, over
whatever you were doing, because a version number had changed rather than
because anybody asked for it.

Nothing asks for a screen here that you did not ask for. How Lodestar Works is
in the menu bar for anyone who wants the deck back, and since the walkthrough
now owns the keyboard for as long as it is up, an unrequested takeover is the
wrong kind of surprise to hand somebody who was in the middle of a sentence.
