Scroll mode goes the way its keys say again. j and d had started running up the page and k and u down it, and G and gg had swapped ends in every window that does not hand Lodestar its scrollbar, which is most of them. Scroll mode had been asking the Mac which way you scroll, and the Mac's answer never applies to a scroll an app makes. It only seemed to, because Scroll Reverser used to turn Lodestar's scrolling over along with your mouse's, and its new version stopped. Every key in scroll mode now goes one way on every Mac, and each one is checked before a release can ship.

A mouse can now scroll apart from the trackpad. macOS keeps one direction for both, which is why Scroll Reverser exists. Mouse scrolling, in General, takes System, Natural or Standard. System leaves things as the Mac has them. The other two turn only the mouse wheel, so the trackpad keeps the Mac's own setting. A mouse app such as Logi Options+ counts as the mouse. A Magic Mouse counts as a trackpad, since it scrolls like one. If Scroll Reverser is still running, Settings says so, because the two together would turn the wheel twice. The health record keeps the direction your fingers moved, before any of this.
