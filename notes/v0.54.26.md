A release that counts how many Macs use Lodestar each day, with no ID, and has its own switch to stop it.

Once a day, after you first use Lodestar, it tells lodestar.vaccone.software that this Mac used it. It sends three things: the Lodestar version, the update channel, and roughly how long ago Lodestar was installed (that day, within a week, within a month, within three months, or longer). It sends no ID, no date and nothing about what you do, so no two days can be tied to the same Mac. The site adds one to that day's totals and does not keep the address the request came from.

Only a deliberate use counts: a key struck under lode, a fix taken in the editor, or words landed by dictation. The count goes out at a random moment up to two hours later, so it never says when you started work, and it is sent at most once a day.

It has its own switch, apart from updates, in Settings under General: Daily count. Turn it off and nothing is sent. A new Mac says so on the first launch window, and the privacy page lists it with everything else that leaves your Mac.
