A release that moves Lodestar's clipboard reader into a part of the app macOS runs on its own.

Each copy is now read by a small service built into Lodestar, the way Apple has apps keep work they cannot wait on apart from themselves. macOS starts it when a copy needs reading and stops it when it is idle. A copy that is slow to read, such as a file from the Finder or something copied on another device, still waits there and never holds up your keys, and a read that stops answering now ends the service so the next copy starts a fresh one.
