The letter O goes back to being an address.

Flipping a layout's orientation now lives on `lode \`, the key wearing the
vertical bar: the key that draws the line flips the layout. It had been on
O since the verb shipped, which spent a letter on a gesture used a handful
of times a month while applications whose names start with O routed around
it forever.

That is the whole change, and the reason it is worth a release is what it
frees. O is a graph letter again: the onboarding proposals will offer it,
the ⌘K card will accept it, and the coach's shorten suggestions can now
price it. X and Z stay where they are — the timeline and undo have earned
their letters — so the advisor's reserved set shrinks to those two.

If your hands know `lode O`, they will find the flip at `lode \` and the
cheat sheet says so. An existing graph is untouched; a letter becoming
available is an invitation, never a migration.
