A release that gives every update a record of its own on this Mac, so Lodestar says what happened to it once, and can say later who decided what and why.

Each release Lodestar is offered now has a record that follows it from download to running, rolled back or refused, across launches and updates. The record is written beside the files Lodestar has always used to hand an update from one version to the next, and those files are kept exactly as they were, so an update to or from an earlier version works as it always has.

"Lodestar has updated" is now said once. It used to be said again at every launch until Lodestar was ready, and twice when both halves of a handover lived long enough to say it. A rollback is said once too, even when the instance that found it closes before it can speak, and it is still said if the record cannot be reached.

A release that failed to start here is still never offered again, and the record now says when it failed and why. If you go back to an older Lodestar by hand and the newer one is offered again, it is applied from the start and its arrival is said again. `lodestar diagnose` lists every release on this Mac with its state, since when, and who moved it last and why.

The record is kept out of Time Machine with the rest of Lodestar's behavioral files, so restoring one Mac onto another never carries over a verdict about a release that never ran there.
