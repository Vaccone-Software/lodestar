A release of fixes for things that could lose your work, and a clipboard that no longer holds up the keyboard.

Lodestar now reads each copy in a small process of its own. A copy that is slow to read, such as a file from the Finder or something copied on another device, used to keep Lodestar waiting for up to three seconds, and your keys waited with it. Now only the reader waits.

Pasting a card from Keep whose saved copy will not open now leaves your clipboard as it was. It used to empty the clipboard first and then say the card could not be read.

The letters the click door shows now stay on the targets they were drawn on. On a busy page a letter could move to another target while you were reading it, so pressing it clicked something else.

In the draft, `lode .` or `lode ⇧.` pressed while ⏎ is still landing no longer turns the microphone on with nothing on screen, and no longer drops your last words. ⏎ now lands words still shown in grey between two recordings, as esc would keep them. Quitting within a second of esc now keeps the draft on the clipboard, where its text used to be lost.

If an update ever has to be rolled back, the previous version is put back before the failed one is removed, so an app is always there to start. A rollback interrupted halfway used to leave half an app, or none.
