When an app holds Secure Input, macOS lets no key reach Lodestar, and every lode gesture goes dead without a word. That is right while you type a password. It is wrong when the app keeps the hold after you have left it, which Chromium browsers do when a password field keeps its focus in a window at the back. Until now the only sign was a keyboard that had stopped working.

Lodestar now says so. When Secure Input stays on for a few seconds in an app that is not in front, a small surface appears where the guide does: the lode key, drawn faded because no press of it can arrive, beside a line naming the app that is holding your keys. Click it and that app comes forward, so you can leave its password field or finish the prompt. When Secure Input ends, the key lights and the line says your keys are back, then it goes. Nothing is said while the holder is in front or the screen is locked, and the record still notes only that Secure Input was on, never which app held it.
